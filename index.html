Murimi Intelligent Hub

On GitHub

A Django platform that connects farmers, landowners, investors and transport drivers in Zimbabwe.

Everyone gets a verified account and their own dashboard. Field officers check people and land before a deal goes ahead, and IDs, title deeds and farm photos are uploaded to private storage for staff to review.

Farm projects have funding targets and milestones, staff post crop prices in US$ and ZiG, and haulage jobs are matched to drivers’ truck sizes. Phone numbers are verified with codes sent over WhatsApp.

The back end has a 21-endpoint JSON API and 40 automated tests.

Python, Django, PostgreSQL, WhatsApp Cloud API, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Chats & Sons

Live, 2025 to now

Website and ordering system for Chats and Sons, my family’s carpentry and joinery workshop in Harare.

Customers browse boards, fittings and furniture, then confirm their order on WhatsApp. The workshop manages the catalogue and incoming orders from an admin side. I took the product photos myself.

It also has a cutting list tool. You enter the parts for a job, like kitchen units or a wardrobe, and it works out how many 2750 × 1830 mm boards of each material to buy, with an allowance for waste.

React, TypeScript, React Router, Firebase Auth, Firestore, Tailwind CSS

Murimi Market

On GitHub

A marketplace where Zimbabwean farmers sell produce directly to buyers at a fair price.

Farmers list what they have, or what will be ready within two weeks, and buyers browse by product and province and make offers. A fair-price meter shows how an offer compares. Buyers can also post what they need, and several farmers can each supply part of it.

Nobody’s phone number is shown until an offer is accepted. It also has ratings and safe order handling, and 122 automated tests.

Python, Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
